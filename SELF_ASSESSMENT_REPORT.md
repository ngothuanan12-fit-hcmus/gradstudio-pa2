## What we did not manage

- **Screenshots of the 3D landing page.** The story is scroll-driven and its height follows the window height. DevTools' *Capture full size screenshot* stretches the window to the whole page, so that capture showed only the first frame and a dark page, with the sections below missing. `index-768.png` and `index-1280.png` are therefore screen-height captures taken while scrolling, joined top to bottom. They show what a reader sees, but they are not single DevTools captures. `index-360.png` and the other six files are normal full-page captures.
- **W3C by script.** The checker refused automated requests, so every page was pasted into *Text input* by hand.
- **Khánh's version of the two inner pages was not used.** It was built from the authors' prototype code, which the rubric does not allow. An rebuilt both pages on the team's own components instead (AI-LOG, 2026-11-11). Khánh's share of the page code is therefore small. His commits are in the history.
- **Not wired yet** (listed in `PROTOTYPE.md`): sending a chat message and *Thanh toán cọc 30%* are disabled buttons with a note, because PA#2 has no server or payment gateway.
- **The management page is not protected.** Anyone can open it by its path. A real version needs a sign-in on the server.

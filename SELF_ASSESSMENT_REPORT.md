## What we did not manage

- **One-commit-per-part for the Admin screens.** G4–G6 came as one large commit, and the G7, G8, G10 commits were split after the work. The AI-LOG says so in both places; we did not rebuild the history to look finer than it was.
- **Product-owner questions still open** (`FRONTEND.md` section 5): the reminder text, what follows a declined proposal, the head count of a class yearbook, whether every package may record extras, whether opening hours limit a proposal, and whether moving a deposit-paid booking that lost the race to a free time holds it. Each is a team assumption, marked as such in the contract.
- **Seed booking GS-1023** has make-up artist Mai on both make-up and shoot at 08:00–12:00, which looks odd when shown.
- **The time fields of `/quan-ly/booking/:id`** show the browser's 12-hour format in an English-language browser, while every other screen writes 24-hour times.
- **Real e-mail and payment** are PA#4: the reminders show the e-mail status the mock reports, and the test gateway is a route of the app.
